# GIRZOCK Technologies: Local SEO Content Strategy

Date: 7 November 2026.

## Principle
GIRZOCK Technologies has **one office, in Thisaiyanvilai** (Tirunelveli District). Every other place is a **service area**: projects run online, with visits when needed. The site says this plainly and never implies a branch office.

## Why there are no separate city pages
The brief allowed location pages only where genuinely useful. Separate pages for each town would repeat the same service text with a different town name. Google treats such pages as doorway pages, and they would help no customer. One Areas We Serve page with real information per district, plus local paragraphs on the service pages, covers these searches honestly.

A dedicated page for one town is worth adding only when there is something specific to say: local clients who agree to be named, local projects, or a regular meeting place.

## Search themes these pages now support
- Website development company in Tirunelveli: website development service and homepage.
- Web design company in Thisaiyanvilai: homepage, About, Areas We Serve.
- Software development company in Tirunelveli: custom software service and homepage.
- Website development services in Thoothukudi and Sattankulam: Areas We Serve and service pages.
- Custom software development in Nagercoil: custom software service and Areas We Serve.
- Web development services in Kanyakumari: Areas We Serve and website development.

## Next steps that do not need code
1. **Google Business Profile:** create or claim it with the exact Thisaiyanvilai address and phone used on the site. Set the service areas to the three districts.
2. **Reviews:** ask real clients for Google reviews. Do not add reviews to the website without permission.
3. **Draft blog posts:** 20 drafts cover local topics (Thoothukudi, Nagercoil, Kanyakumari, Thisaiyanvilai). Review, expand and publish them one at a time; do not publish them as they are.
4. **Search Console:** after launch, watch which towns appear in search queries. Strengthen those pages first.

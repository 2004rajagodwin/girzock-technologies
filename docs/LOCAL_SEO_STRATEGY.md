**Date:** 7 November 2026

## 1. The one rule

GIRZOCK Technologies has **one place of business: Thisaiyanvilai, Tirunelveli District.** Every other town is a **service area**, reached online and by visits when needed. The website, the structured data and any directory profile must say exactly this. No branch offices, no invented street addresses, no fake listings.

## 2. Target places

| Priority | Places | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Thisaiyanvilai** (the base), **Tirunelveli** (district headquarters and the most searched place name), **Sattankulam** (neighbouring town in Thoothukudi District) | Nearest customers, and the searches competitors ignore (see the competitor report) |
| Districts | Tirunelveli, Thoothukudi, Kanniyakumari | The region served |
| Larger towns | Palayamkottai, Valliyur, Nagercoil, Thoothukudi, Tiruchendur, Kovilpatti, Marthandam | Real demand centres within reach |
| Nearby beyond Tamil Nadu | Thiruvananthapuram | Already named as a service area |

**Spelling:** Google treats variants as related. The site uses the common English forms Thisaiyanvilai, Kanniyakumari or Kanyakumari, and Thoothukudi, which also appears as Tuticorin. Wikipedia spells the town "Thisayanvilai". Use one form consistently in the Google Business Profile, matching the website: **Thisaiyanvilai**.

**Tenkasi:** Alangulam has been in Tenkasi District since 2019. Tenkasi is not targeted, because you have not said you serve it.

Place names are not repeated in every paragraph, and there are no near-identical town pages.

## 4. What only you can do (largest effect first)

1. **Google Business Profile:**
   - Create or claim it with the exact business name, the Thisaiyanvilai address and the phone number `+91 6379362330`.
   - Choose a primary category such as "Software company" or "Website designer".
   - Set the service areas to the three districts.
   - Add real photos, the opening hours and the website `https://girzock.com`.
   - Then send me the profile link. It goes into Website Settings and the structured data (`sameAs`, plus `hasMap`).
2. **Street address and PIN code:** if you are comfortable publishing them, send them to me. The structured data then gains `streetAddress` and `postalCode`. They are not stored today, and none have been invented.
3. **Reviews:** ask real clients for reviews on the Google Business Profile. Do not copy reviews onto the website without permission, and do not add star ratings to the structured data.
4. **Directory profiles:**
   - Create profiles on 3 to 5 directories that competitors use: IndiaMART, GoodFirms, TechBehemoths, The Manifest, Clutch, Justdial.
   - Use the same name, address and phone everywhere.
   - These are honest citations, not bought links.
5. **LinkedIn and Facebook:** both are now linked in the footer and the structured data. Post occasional real updates. **Check that the Facebook link opens your page:** it could not be checked automatically.

## 6. How to measure

After launch, use Google Search Console (the verification code is already in the site's head):
- Performance → Queries, filtered by place names, shows which towns bring visits.
- Strengthen the pages behind those queries first.

In the Google Business Profile, Performance shows calls, direction requests and website clicks.

No ranking can be promised. Local results depend heavily on the Google Business Profile, reviews and distance, which a website alone cannot control.

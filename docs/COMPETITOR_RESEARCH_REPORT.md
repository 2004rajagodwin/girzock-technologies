# Competitor Research Report

**Date:** 7 November 2026

**Method:**
- web searches for the target phrases in the brief, on a US-based search tool (see the limits below);
- reading the result pages;
- opening three competitor websites to look at their structure.

**Rules followed:**
- Only companies that appeared in the results are named.
- No competitor text, branding or layout has been copied.
- Claims made by competitors about themselves are reported as their claims, not as facts.

> **Limits.** The search tool runs from the United States, not from Tirunelveli. Google's local results and Maps listings near Thisaiyanvilai may differ. Repeat the key searches from a phone in the area, signed out of Google, before acting on rankings. No paid SEO tool was used, so there are no search volume or backlink counts here.

## 2. What the competitors do

### Page structure and service coverage

**Advetri** (advetri.com, Tirunelveli):
- **Menu:** Home, About, Services, Blogs, Contact.
- **Services:** one combined services page. The homepage "Learn More" buttons point to `#`, so its individual services have no real pages.
- **Location pages:** there are state-level pages such as "web development company in Tamil Nadu".
- **Content problems:** placeholder text ("Lorem ipsum", "This is the heading") is visible on the homepage, and the statistics counters showed 0 when fetched.
- **Footer:** links to unrelated outside repositories.

**Zenvic Soft** (zenvicsoft.com, Tirunelveli): a plain fetch of the homepage returned only the company name. The content is drawn by JavaScript, so search engines that do not run scripts see very little.

**Patron Services** (patronservices.in):
- **Approach:** one page per service per town, for example "Software Development Company in Nagercoil".
- **Template:** each page has about 800 to 1,000 words with the same headings: local details, why choose us, use cases, pricing guidance, FAQs and nearby cities.
- **Repetition:** most of the text is generic, with the town name repeated, plus a line of stacked keywords.

**Directory profiles:** most firms in Thoothukudi and Nagercoil appear mainly through IndiaMART, GoodFirms, TechBehemoths, The Manifest and Clutch rather than through their own sites.

### Keyword targeting, titles and descriptions

- **Exact match is the norm.** Titles copy the search phrase exactly ("Software Development Company in Nagercoil | …").
- **Self-written ranking posts.** Several firms rank by publishing posts on free Blogspot sites with titles such as "Web Design Companies in Tirunelveli". These posts are self-promotional lists.
- **The smallest towns are ignored.** No competitor targets Thisaiyanvilai or Sattankulam.

### Content depth and originality

- Most firm websites in the results have short service text.
- The longest local pages found (Patron Services) are templated across towns.
- No competitor in the results published case material or guides specific to a district.

### Local SEO approach

- **Directories:** location appears through directory listings and town names in titles.
- **Addresses:** the firms that show a street address do so in their footer and directory profiles.
- **Google Maps:** a search tool like this one cannot see Maps listings. Check them by hand.

### Internal linking

- Advetri's service buttons lead nowhere (`#`).
- Patron Services links each town page to nearby towns and other services in the same town. This is structured, but repetitive.

### Blogs

- Advetri has a blog section, but no posts were visible on its homepage.
- Azasoft and Advetri publish on separate Blogspot sites rather than on their own domains, so those posts build little authority for their main sites.

### Backlinks (from public pages only)

The links these firms visibly have come from:
- business directories: IndiaMART, GoodFirms, TechBehemoths, The Manifest, Clutch, Sortlist;
- LinkedIn company pages;
- listing articles (Housing.com, TraffiCtail);
- their own Blogspot posts;
- job boards.

No measured backlink data was available.

### Technical issues seen

- Content drawn only by JavaScript (Zenvic).
- Placeholder text left on a live homepage, and `#` links (Advetri).
- Keyword-stuffed lines on town pages (Patron Services).

## 4. Opportunities, in order of likely effect

1. **Google Business Profile (you).** Create or claim it with the real Thisaiyanvilai address, the hours and the services. This is the most important single step for "near me" and Maps searches. Use exactly the same name, phone and town as the website.
2. **Honest directory profiles (you).** Most competitors are found through IndiaMART, GoodFirms, TechBehemoths, The Manifest and Clutch. Real profiles on 3 to 5 of them, with the same name, address and phone, give genuine citations and links. **Do not buy links or create fake listings.**
3. **Own the nearest towns.** Nobody targets Thisaiyanvilai or Sattankulam. The site already names them naturally (home, About, Contact, Areas We Serve, service pages). Keep doing that through real content, such as a blog post about how businesses around Thisaiyanvilai and Sattankulam can get online. **Do not** create separate near-identical town pages; that is the template pattern seen at Patron Services.
4. **Fill the content gaps competitors leave:**
   - practical local guides, such as "what a website costs a small business in Tamil Nadu" (already published);
   - questions owners ask before buying;
   - honest comparisons such as WordPress or custom (published).

   Section 8 of `SEO_CONTENT_OPTIMIZATION_REPORT.md` lists the next topics.
5. **Real proof:** client-approved case studies with outcomes, and reviews on the Google Business Profile, once you have them. None should be invented.
6. **LinkedIn company page:** now linked from the site and from the structured data. Post occasional project updates there.

## 5. What not to copy

- Lists of "top companies" written by a company about itself.
- Keyword lines such as "Software Company IT Software Company".
- Town pages that differ only in the town name.
- Counters or star ratings without real data behind them.
- Placeholder text, and buttons that go nowhere.
